Mogu Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon sources (in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard publications). No invented words are included. IPA transcriptions are approximate, based on the romanized spelling. Green = high confidence; Sienna = inferred morpheme; gray = uncertain gloss. Light yellow background = new additions.

Chinese localization data: 魔古 Mógǔ for “Mogu,” 魔古山 Mógǔshān for “Mogu’shan,” 雷神 Léishén for “Lei Shen” provides additional gloss evidence; these are translations, not necessarily the original source, but they strongly support the inferred morpheme meanings.

Nouns

WordIPAGlossSource / Notes
Mogu/ˈmoʊɡuː/(uncertain; possibly “demon-ancient” or “magic-ancient”)The race name. Self-designation. Chinese 魔古 = “demon/magic” + “ancient,” but this may be a Chinese rendering, not the original etymology.
Lei/leɪ/“thunder” (inferred)From Lei Shen = “Thunder King.” Chinese 雷 (léi) = “thunder” confirms this gloss.
Shen/ʃɛn/“god” / “deity” / “king” (inferred)From Lei Shen = “Thunder King.” Chinese 神 (shén) = “god/deity” confirms this gloss. Broader than “king” alone; implies divine authority.
Shan/ʃɑn/“mountain” (inferred; corrected from “palace/mountain”)From Mogu’shan place names. Chinese 山 (shān) = “mountain” confirms this gloss. Mogu’shan = “Mogu Mountain,” not “Mogu Palace.”
Qon/kɔn/(inferred; possibly “iron” or “strength”)From Iron Qon; “Qon” may correspond to “Iron.”
Qiang/tʃjɑŋ/(inferred; possibly “strong” or “ruthless”)From Qiang the Merciless; may correspond to the epithet. Also a Mogu dynasty name.
Zian/ziːˈɑn/(inferred; possibly “shadow” or “dark”)From Zian of the Endless Shadow; may correspond to the epithet.
Meng/mɛŋ/(inferred; possibly “mad” or “fierce”)From Meng the Demented; may correspond to the epithet.
Xin/ʃɪn/(inferred; possibly “weapon” or “blade”)From Xin the Weaponmaster; may correspond to the epithet.
Wai/waɪ/(unknown)From the Wai dynasty, one of three confirmed Mogu dynasties. Meaning unknown.
Feng/fɛŋ/(inferred; possibly “accursed” or “wind”)From Feng the Accursed. Name-epithet pattern. Chinese 风 (fēng) = “wind,” but the epithet suggests “accursed.”
Kuai/kwaɪ/(inferred; possibly “brigand” or “swift”)From Kuai the Brigand. Name-epithet pattern.
Hai/haɪ/(inferred; possibly “unbreakable” or “sea”)From Hai the Unbreakable. Name-epithet pattern. Chinese 海 (hǎi) = “sea,” but epithet suggests “unbreakable.”
Ming/mɪŋ/(inferred; possibly “bright” or “cunning”)From Ming the Cunning. Name-epithet pattern. Chinese 明 (míng) = “bright/clear.”

Clan Names

WordIPAGlossSource / Notes
Shan’ze/ʃɑnˈzɛ/Mogu clanTownlong Steppes, Jade Forest, Isle of Thunder. Compound: shan (“mountain”?) + ze (unknown). Led by Shan Bu. Specialists in lightning and soul-binding magic.
Baruk/bɑˈruk/Mogu clanRose after Lei Shen’s defeat. Stubborn and prideful. Use spirits of the dead to animate statues. Possibly monomorphemic.
Dokani/doʊˈkɑːni/Mogu clanRose after Lei Shen’s defeat. Masters of blood magic and blood golems. Possibly dok + ani or monomorphemic.
Zan-Tien/zɑnˈtiːɛn/Mogu clanRose after Lei Shen’s defeat. Specialists in lightning/storm magic. Compound: zan + tien. Chinese 天 (tiān) = “heaven/sky” may gloss tien.
Rajani/rɑˈdʒɑːni/Mogu clanAppeared in BfA patch 8.3 (N’Zoth assault on the Vale). Allied with the player against N’Zoth. Possibly raj + ani or monomorphemic.
