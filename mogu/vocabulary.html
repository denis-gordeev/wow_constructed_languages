Mogu Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon. Green = high confidence; Gold = inferred morpheme; gray = uncertain gloss. Dark brown background = new additions from MoP expansion.

Chinese localization data: 魔古 Mógǔ = "Mogu," 魔古山 Mógǔshān = "Mogu'shan," 雷神 Léishén = "Lei Shen." These are translations, not necessarily original etymology, but they strongly support the inferred morpheme meanings.

Nouns

WordIPAGlossSource / Notes
Mogu/ˈmoʊɡuː/(uncertain; possibly "demon-ancient" or "magic-ancient")The race name. Self-designation. Chinese 魔古 = "demon/magic" + "ancient."
Lei/leɪ/"thunder"From Lei Shen = "Thunder King." Chinese 雷 (léi) confirms.
Shen/ʃɛn/"god" / "deity" / "ruler"From Lei Shen. Chinese 神 (shén) confirms. Broader than "king"; implies divine authority.
Shan/ʃɑn/"mountain"From Mogu'shan. Chinese 山 (shān) confirms. Mogu'shan = "Mogu Mountain."
Qon/kɔn/(inferred; possibly "iron" or "strength")From Iron Qon; "Qon" may correspond to "Iron."
Qiang/tʃjɑŋ/(inferred; possibly "strong" or "ruthless")From Qiang the Merciless; also a Mogu dynasty name.
Zian/ziːˈɑn/(inferred; possibly "shadow" or "dark")From Zian of the Endless Shadow.
Meng/mɛŋ/(inferred; possibly "mad" or "fierce")From Meng the Demented.
Xin/ʃɪn/(inferred; possibly "weapon" or "blade")From Xin the Weaponmaster.
Feng/fɛŋ/(inferred; possibly "accursed" or "wind")From Feng the Accursed. Chinese 风 (fēng) = "wind."
Kuai/kwaɪ/(inferred; possibly "brigand" or "swift")From Kuai the Brigand.
Hai/haɪ/(inferred; possibly "unbreakable" or "sea")From Hai the Unbreakable. Chinese 海 (hǎi) = "sea."
Ming/mɪŋ/(inferred; possibly "bright" or "cunning")From Ming the Cunning. Chinese 明 (míng) = "bright/clear."
