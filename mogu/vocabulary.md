# Mogu Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon sources (in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard publications). No invented words are included. IPA transcriptions are approximate, based on the romanized spelling.

Chinese localization data (魔古 Mógǔ for "Mogu," 魔古山 Mógǔshān for "Mogu'shan," 雷神 Léishén for "Lei Shen") provides additional gloss evidence; these are translations, not necessarily the original source, but they strongly support the inferred morpheme meanings.
