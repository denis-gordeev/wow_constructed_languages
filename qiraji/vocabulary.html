The Ahn- Element: Qiraji ↔ Nerubian

Key finding: The Ahn- element appears in both qiraji (Ahn'qiraj) and nerubian (Ahn'kahet) toponyms, with the same structural pattern (*Ahn-* + apostrophe + second morpheme). Both species descend from the Aqir, making a shared Aqir substrate morpheme the most likely explanation. Confidence: Low–Medium.
