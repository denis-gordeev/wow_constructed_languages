# Nature / Druidic Vocabulary

This document compiles vocabulary related to druidic communication, nature spirits, and the "language of the wild" in World of Warcraft. Unlike other languages in this collection, "Nature" or "Druidic" is not a constructed language with attested words — it is a communication system between druids, nature spirits, and the natural world. The attested corpus consists entirely of proper names, titles, and inferred morphemes from druidic traditions.

No invented words are included. All entries are attested from in-game dialogue, quest text, or official Blizzard sources.

## Notes

The "language of nature" or "Druidic" is not attested as a distinct constructed language in World of Warcraft. Druids in WoW communicate in their native languages (Darnassian, Taur-ahe, Common) rather than a separate druidic tongue. The druidic tradition is transmitted through titles (Shan'do / Thero'shan), place names, and nature spirit names — none of which constitute a grammar or distinct linguistic system.

The closest analogue to a "nature language" in WoW is the communication between druids and nature spirits, which is depicted as an intuitive or magical understanding rather than a verbal language. The Emerald Dream may have its own form of communication, but this is not attested in linguistic terms.

Some Darnassian vocabulary (tel "earth", falah "balance", drassil "crown") is deeply tied to druidic concepts and may represent the closest thing to a "druidic lexicon" in WoW canon. These are Darnassian words, however, not a separate language.
