Attested Phrases and Sentences

PhraseIPAGlossSource / Notes
Achusenmen osadi djahi kahun Ammunae /ɑxusɛnmɛn osɑdi dʒɑhi kɑhun ɑmunɑe/ (untranslated) Inscription on the Staff of Ammunae archaeology artifact. The longest attested Tol'vir text. Ammunae = a titan keeper of the Halls of Origination.
Kadesh! Osadi! Satekh! /kɑdɛʃ osɑdi sɑtɛk/ "Swing! Swing! Block!" Engraved Scimitar Hilt. Ramkahen Sergeant drill. Kadesh = a swing, Osadi = another swing, Satekh = a block.
Murkash! /murkɑʃ/ (battle cry; untranslated) Said by General Husam in combat.
