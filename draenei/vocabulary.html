M'uru/ˈmuːruː/(name of a Naaru)Dark Naaru; captured by Blood Knights in TBC, drained of Light, sacrificed to reignite the Sunwell. Most narratively significant dark Naaru.
O'ros/ˈoʊrɒs/(name of a Naaru)Naaru aboard the Exodar (TBC).
L'ura/ˈluːrɑː/(name of a Naaru)Dark naaru. Originally aboard the Xenedar on Mac'Aree (Argus, Legion); corrupted by the Void. Absorbed into Alleria Windrunner during the Argus campaign. Alleria used L'ura's essence to kill the corrupted naaru T'era at Nexus-Point Mid'Ar in the Voidstorm. Released by Xal'atath during the Midnight Voidspire confrontation; fired a void beam into the Sunwell, corrupting it into the Darkwell. Destroyed by combined elven forces at the Darkwell. Initial-apostrophe name following the naaru naming template: L' + -ura; see Draenei grammar Investigation 1.
T'era/ˈtɛrə/(name of a Naaru)Naaru captured by the Shadowguard in the Voidstorm (Midnight). Salhadaar fully corrupted T'era into a dark naaru at Nexus-Point Mid'Ar. Alleria Windrunner used the essence of L'ura inside her to kill the corrupted T'era. Initial-apostrophe name following the naaru naming template: T' + -era; see Draenei grammar Investigation 1. Parallels M'uru: both were Light naaru captured and corrupted by hostile forces (Blood Knights vs. Shadowguard).
Naaru/ˈnɑːru/(the beings of Light)May not be a Draenei word per se; possibly self-designation.

Phrases (Argus / WoD)

No new glossed phrases are attested from Argus or Warlords of Draenor content. Draenei NPCs on Argus speak Common in-game. The Argus storyline provides extensive lore about the Eredar/Draenei split but no new Draenei-language sentences.
Note on IPA conventions: Transcriptions follow standard IPA. Primary stress is marked with ˈ before the stressed syllable; secondary stress with ˌ. Vowels follow General American conventions. The apostrophe in words like Man'ari, Sha'tar, and Oshu'gun likely indicates a morpheme boundary rather than a glottal stop. Entries in gray italic indicate uncertain glosses; entries in blue indicate inferred morphemes; rows with light indigo background are new additions from Argus, Broken Isles, and Legion content.
