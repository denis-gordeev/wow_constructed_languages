# Draenei Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon sources (in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard publications). No invented words are included.

## Nouns

| Word | Gloss | Source / Notes |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Draenor | "Exiles' Refuge" | Draenei name for the planet. Compound of *draen* + *or*. |
| Karabor | "Temple of Karabor" | Place name; the Black Temple. Contains *kara* ("temple"). |
| Shattrath | "Dwelling of Light" | City name in Terokkar Forest. |
| Auchindoun | (uncertain; possibly "resting place") | Place name; draenei crypt city. |
| Oshu'gun | (uncertain) | Place name; the crashed draenei vessel in Nagrand. |
| Telredor | (uncertain) | Place name; draenei settlement in Zangarmarsh. |
| Telaar | (uncertain) | Place name; draenei settlement in Nagrand. |
| Tuurem | (uncertain) | Place name; draenei settlement in Terokkar Forest. |
| Farahlon | (uncertain) | Place name; region on Draenor (now Netherstorm). |
| Xenedar | (uncertain) | Place name; draenei vessel on Argus. |
| Vindicaar | (uncertain) | Place name; draenei vessel used in the Legion campaign. |
| Argus | (uncertain) | Draenei/Eredar homeworld. May predate the Draenei/Eredar split. |

## People / Groups

| Word | Gloss | Source / Notes |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Draenei | "Exiled Ones" | Self-designation. Compound of *draen* + *ei*. |
| Eredar | (original species name) | Pre-split name; now refers to the demon-corrupted branch. |
| Man'ari | "wrong" / "unnatural" / "corrupt" / "damned" | Draenei term for Eredar who joined the Burning Legion. |
| Krokul | "Broken" / "devolved" | Term for Draenei mutated by fel energy. |
