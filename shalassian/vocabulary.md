# Shalassian Vocabulary

## Language & Identity

| Word | Translation | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Shalassian | The Nightborne language | Named after the Shal'dorei |
| Shal'dorei | Children of the Twilight / Nightborne | Self-designation of the Nightborne |
| Shal | Twilight / arcane | (inferred) from Shal'dorei, Shal'Aran |
| dorei | Children of | Shared with Darnassian (Kaldorei, Quel'dorei) |
