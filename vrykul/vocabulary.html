8. Phonological Observations

Key features of Vrykul phonology:
  • Heavy initial consonant clusters — Vr-, Sk-, Hl-, Gj-, Mj-, Ym-, Hr-, St- are common
  • Gj → /ʃ/ — Canon: Captain Adams confirms Gj in Gjalerbron = /ʃ/
  • First-syllable stress — Consistent with Germanic/Old Norse pattern
  • Consonant gemination — Attested in Skarvald, Sjonnir (pre-aspiration or gemination)
  • Rhotic-heavy — Alveolar tap or trill [ɾ/r], pervasive in names
