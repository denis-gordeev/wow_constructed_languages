## 8. Phonological Observations

### Consonant Distribution

- **Initial clusters**: Vr-, Sk-, Hl-, Gj-, Mj-, Ym-, Hr-, Sn-, Br-, Gr-, Fl-, Dr-, St-
- **Medial clusters**: -lg-, -ld-, -rn-, -rv-, -lv-, -nh-, -th-, -dh-, -sk-
- **Final clusters**: -rn, -ld, -lf, -ng, -nn, -rr

### Phonotactic Constraints

- Syllable structure appears to be (C)(C)V(C)(C), consistent with Old Norse
- No vowel-initial words are common; most names begin with consonant clusters
- The language permits complex onsets (3-consonant clusters in "Str-" as in Sturmpyk, Sturmvyn)

### Sound Correspondences with Old Norse

| Vrykul | Old Norse | Meaning |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Val- | Val- | Slain/slaughter |
| -heim | -heimr | Home/realm |
| -jar | -jar | Army/host |
| -garde | garðr | Enclosure |
| Jotun- | Jǫtun- | Giant |
| Geirr- | Geirr- | Spear |
| -vif | vif | Wife |
| Drott- | Drótt- | Band/host |
| -inn | -inn | Definite article |
| Gj- → /ʃ/ | Gj- → /j/ | Divergent development |

### Notable Divergences from Old Norse

1. **Gj → /ʃ/**: Where Old Norse developed Gj into /j/, Vrykul developed it into /ʃ/. This is a unique sound change not found in real Norse.
2. **-halas vs. -hǫll**: Vrykul uses -halas where Old Norse uses -hǫll (hall). The Vrykul form may preserve an older or parallel development.
3. **-korn vs. -korn**: The -korn suffix in clan names (Winterskorn, Tideskorn) uses the word for "grain/seed" metaphorically as "chosen/kin", a semantic extension not standard in Old Norse.

## 9. Relationship to Other WoW Languages

### Vrykul — Titan Connection

The vrykul are titan-forged (iron vrykul), and their language descends from Titan. Key shared features:
- Place names with structural suffixes (-heim, -garde) parallel Titan facility naming (Uld- prefix)
- The "Val-" root exists in both: Vrykul Val'kyr and Titan Val'anyr (both relate to "chosen/slaken")
- Structural complexity of compounds suggests a shared compounding tradition

### Vrykul — Dwarven Connection

Both descend from titan-forged:
- Vrykul from iron vrykul, Dwarves from Earthen
- Both may share the "Uld-" prefix in some place names (though this is primarily Titan)
- The compounding pattern (modifier-head) may be shared

### Vrykul — Common (Human) Connection

This is the most significant relationship:
- Humans are the direct descendants of vrykul ("runt" children)
- The wiki notes that Common shares many similar words with Vrykul
- Common, Dwarven, Gnomish, and Vrykul all share vocabulary, likely through their common Titan ancestor
- "Tirisfal" is a Vrykul word (Tyr's Fall) that became a Common place name, showing direct borrowing
- Over millennia, human language would have simplified Vrykul phonology (loss of initial clusters, simplification of case system)
