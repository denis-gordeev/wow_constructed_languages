Note on IPA conventions: Phonetic transcriptions follow standard IPA usage. The apostrophe (') in Zandali words (e.g. Zul'Gurub, Vol'jin) represents a morpheme boundary, not a glottal stop. Stress is marked with the primary stress symbol (ˈ). Vowel length is indicated by the length mark (ː). Transcriptions are based on apparent pronunciation patterns from voice-acted dialogue and are approximate; Zandali phonology is not fully documented in official sources.
Note on BfA sources: The Battle for Azeroth expansion (2018) provided the most extensive Zandali vocabulary in canon, with the Zandalar continent featuring fully voice-acted troll NPCs, loa storylines, and the Zandalari troll allied race quest chain. Many terms in the BfA additions section overlap with earlier sections (e.g. Pa'ku, Krag'wa, Gonk, Bwonsamdi) as these loa were first named in earlier lore but received expanded roles and context in BfA.

Midnight Expansion (2026): Amani Names

Amani troll names and Zandali terms attested in the Midnight expansion, set in Quel'Thalas and Zul'Aman.

Word / PhraseIPAMeaningSource / Notes
Zul'jan/zʊlˈdʒɑːn/Amani commander (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Zul- ("great") + -jan. The -jan element is new; possibly a shortened form of a personal name. Uncle is Loa Speaker Kinduru. Will lead a new quest line in patch 12.1.0
Zul'jarra/zʊlˈdʒɑːrə/Chieftain of the Amani tribe; Zul'jan's sister (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Zul- ("great") + -jarra. The -a ending is consistent with feminine naming across WoW languages. Earned the title Hash'ey by uniting the Amani tribes and gaining blessings of four loa of Zul'Aman. Daughter of Zarama (former chieftain, killed by Kol'anji)
Hash'ey/hɑːʃˈeɪ/Amani title meaning "unifier" (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Hash' + -ey. First attested Zandali title with a confirmed semantic gloss. Earned by completing trials and gaining loa blessings; implies Hash' = "unite/gather" and -ey = agentive suffix ("one who does X"). Confidence: Low–Moderate for this decomposition
Mor'duun/mɔːrˈduːn/Leader of Twilight's Blade (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Mor' + -duun. Language affiliation uncertain — could be Zandali, Shath'Yar-influenced, or Common. The -duun element is new. Killed by the united Amani army at the Broken Throne
Zarama/zɑˈrɑːmɑ/Former Amani chieftain; Zul'jarra's mother (Midnight)No apostrophe; -a ending consistent with feminine naming. Ruled the Amani for decades before stepping down after being wounded by gnarldin. Killed by Kol'anji in a leadership challenge
Kol'anji/kɔlˈɑːndʒɪ/Vilebranch chieftain (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Kol' + -anji. Challenged Zarama's leadership of the Amani Empire; killed Zarama before being struck down by Zul'jarra
Man'ye/mɑnˈjɛ/Amani royal family member (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Man' + -ye. Received blessings from Loa Speaker Kinduru before the invasion of Quel'Thalas
Kinduru/kɪnˈduːruː/Loa Speaker; Zul'jan's uncle (Midnight)No apostrophe. Title "Loa Speaker" is a Common-language rendering of a Zandali role. Will appear in patch 12.1.0 quest line with Zul'jan
Pel'na Torntusk/pɛlˈnɑ/Revantusk leader (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: Pel' + -na. "Torntusk" is a Common-language surname. Led the Revantusk to rejoin the Amani at Nalorakk's command
Loa Speaker Nanea/nɑˈneɪə/Loa Speaker serving Nalorakk (Midnight)No apostrophe; short name. "Loa Speaker" is a Common-language title. Seemingly killed by Zul'jarra's forces but actually granted Nalorakk's mercy and rejoined the Amani
Loa Speaker Brek/brɛk/Loa Speaker tending Jan'alai's eggs (Midnight, Patch 12.0.7)No apostrophe; short name. "Loa Speaker" is a Common-language title. Assists with hatching Jan'alai's new clutch of eggs
hash'ura/hɑːʃˈuːrɑ/Zul'jarra's followers/bodyguards (Midnight)Apostrophe-marked compound: hash' + -ura. Lowercase usage suggests it is a common noun, not a proper name — potential attested Zandali common noun. If Hash' = "unite/gather" (from Hash'ey "unifier"), then hash'ura could = "those who unite" or "the united ones" (with -ura = collective suffix). Confidence: Very Low–Low for this decomposition
