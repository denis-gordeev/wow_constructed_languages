# Zandali Vocabulary

All entries are attested from in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard sources. No invented words are included.

## Nouns — People and Tribes

| Word | Meaning | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Zandali | The Troll language; also the Zandalari tribe name | Self-designation of both language and people |
| Gurubashi | People of the Jungle; the Gurubashi tribe | From in-game lore |
| Amani | The Amani tribe | Forest troll tribe |
| Drakkari | The Drakkari tribe | Ice troll tribe |
| Farraki | The Farraki / Sandfury tribe | Desert troll tribe |
| Zandalari | People of Zandalar | The ruling troll tribe |
| Atal'ai | Devoted Ones / Chosen Ones | Priests of Hakkar |
| Loa | Troll spirits/gods | Beings worshipped by trolls |
