# Ethereal Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon (NPC dialogue, quest text, in-game text, official Blizzard sources) unless marked. Inferences are marked with [inferred].

The Ethereal language is spoken by the ethereals — energy beings from the shattered world of K'aresh — and the brokers, a related species from the same homeworld. Their language has no confirmed relationship to the elven, titan-forged, or Aqir-descended language families.

## 3. Attested Phrases

**No constructed-language ethereal phrases are attested.** All ethereal NPCs speak Common (English) in-game. No ethereal-language dialogue, battle cries, or incantations have been recorded.

This parallels the nerubian and qiraji situations — species with no attested constructed-language dialogue.

## 5. The *-esh* / *-aresh* Suffix: A Potential Locative

The element *-esh* appears in two major place names:

| Name | Context | Decomposition |
|------|---------|---------------|
| *K'aresh* | Ethereal homeworld | *K'* + *-aresh* or *K'ar-* + *-esh* |
| *Tazavesh* | Broker capital | *Tazav-* + *-esh* or *Ta-* + *-zavesh* |

If *-esh* = "world" or "realm" or "home":
- *K'aresh* = "[K'ar]-world" or "[K']-home/realm"
- *Tazavesh* = "[Tazav]-world" or "[Tazav]-home/realm"

The *-aresh* form in *K'aresh* could be an extended variant of *-esh* with a linking vowel: *-a-resh* or *-ar-esh*. Alternatively, the root could be *-resh* with different prefixes.

**Confidence: Low–Moderate.** Two data points with consistent semantics (both are realms/worlds), but the possibility of coincidence cannot be excluded.

## 6. The *Nexus-* Title System

The ethereals use a hierarchical title system rendered in Common:

| Title | Attested Holders | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Nexus-King | *Salhadaar* | Highest rank; ruler of K'aresh |
| Nexus-Prince | *Shaffar*, *Haramad* | High nobility; likely a rank below Nexus-King |

The *Nexus-* prefix is a Common-language compound meaning "connection point" or "center of power." It may translate an ethereal-language title element. If the ethereal language had a native title equivalent to "Nexus," it is not attested.

The title hierarchy suggests a political structure:
- **Nexus-King** = sovereign ruler
- **Nexus-Prince** = high nobility / regional governors

The *-King* / *-Prince* elements are Common translations. The ethereal originals are unknown.

## 8. The Broker–Ethereal Linguistic Relationship

Ethereals and brokers are closely related species from K'aresh. The brokers may be a subspecies or cultural variant of ethereals, or they may be a separate but related species. Linguistic evidence for their relationship:

**Shared features:**
- Both use apostrophe-marked morpheme boundaries (*Ve'nari*, *K'aresh*)
- Both share the *-esh* place-name element (*K'aresh*, *Tazavesh*)
- Both originate from K'aresh, implying a shared or closely related language

**Differences:**
- Broker names tend to be shorter (*Ve'nari*, *Tazavesh*) compared to ethereal names (*Salhadaar*, *Haramad*, *Shaffar*)
- The broker *Ve'* prefix is not attested in ethereal names
- Brokers have a mercantile cultural focus (Tazavesh is a trading hub), while ethereals have a more martial/political focus

**Assessment:** Ethereals and brokers likely speak dialects of the same language, with the broker dialect showing shorter name forms and possibly more compact morphological processes. This is consistent with their shared origin on K'aresh.

**Confidence: Low–Moderate.** The shared *-esh* element and shared apostrophe convention suggest a genetic relationship, but the data is insufficient for firm conclusions.

The English word "ethereal" and the WoW race designation "ethereal" likely derive from an in-universe root. If the Consortium faction name "Ethereum" = *Ethere-* + *-um*, this would suggest:
- *Ethere-* = the self-designation root (cf. "Ethereal" as the species name)

This parallels how *Nerub-* appears in *Nerubian* and *Azjol-Nerub* as a self-designation root.

**Confidence: Very Low.** The self-designation may simply be a Common-language rendering with no genuine ethereal-language morpheme content.

## 11. Phonological Observations

### Sound Inventory (Observed from Attested Names)

**Consonants**: b, d, f, h, j, k, l, m, n, r, s, sh (/ʃ/), t, v, z, zh (/ʒ/)

**Vowels**: a, e, i, o, u

### Key Phonological Features

1. **Apostrophe as morpheme boundary**: Consistent with nerubian and Shath'Yar. *K'aresh*, *Ve'nari* both use apostrophe boundaries. This may reflect a shared WoW orthographic convention for "alien" names, or it could indicate genuine morphological structure.

2. **Long vowel sequences**: *Salhadaar* contains the sequence /ɑː/, written *-aa-*. This could reflect an ethereal geminate vowel or a stress-marking convention. If *-daar* = "king/lord," the long vowel may mark the stressed syllable.

3. **Sibilant preference**: Names like *Shaffar*, *Tazavesh*, *K'aresh* feature prominent sibilants (/ʃ/, /s/, /z/). This is consistent with the ethereals' "whispery" nature as energy beings.

4. **No initial consonant clusters**: Unlike nerubian (*Krix'vizk*, *Vrrix*), ethereal names do not feature complex initial clusters. Names are predominantly CVCV or CVC patterns.

5. **Syllable structure**: Tends toward (C)V(C), simpler than nerubian:
   - CV: *Azir*
   - CVC: *Ameer*
   - CVC.CV: *Shaffar*, *Haramad*
   - CV.CV: *Ve'nari*
   - CVC.V.CVC: *Salhadaar*
   - CV.CV.CVC: *Tazavesh*

6. **Post-vocalic /ʃ/ (sh) frequency**: High. *K'aresh*, *Tazavesh*, *Shaffar* all contain /ʃ/. This may reflect Blizzard's design aesthetic for ethereal names, evoking "shimmering" or "whispering" sounds appropriate for energy beings.

### Comparison with Nerubian Phonology

| Feature | Ethereal | Nerubian | Analysis |
|---------|----------|----------|----------|
| Apostrophe usage | Present (*K'aresh*, *Ve'nari*) | Extremely consistent (*Anub'azal*) | Both mark morpheme boundaries; ethereal usage less consistent |
| Initial clusters | Absent | Present (*Krik'thir*, *Vrrix*) | Ethereal has simpler syllable structure |
| Geminate consonants | Possible (*-ff-* in *Shaffar*, *-aa-* in *Salhadaar*) | Absent (*-nax*, not *-naxx*) | Ethereal may retain geminates that nerubian simplified; opposite pattern from qiraji |
| Sibilant density | Moderate-High (*K'aresh*, *Tazavesh*) | Moderate (*Nizrek*, *Krix'vizk*) | Both use sibilants prominently |
| /ks/ (x) frequency | Absent from attested names | High (*Ulgrax*, *Ovi'nax*) | Ethereal lacks the harsh /ks/ cluster |

### Comparison with Shath'Yar Phonology

| Feature | Ethereal | Shath'Yar | Analysis |
|---------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Apostrophe usage | Present (*K'aresh*) | Very consistent (*N'Zoth*, *Yogg-Saron*) | Both use apostrophe boundaries |
| Void connection | Partial (Shadowguard are Void-corrupted) | Core (language of the Old Gods) | Some ethereals have Void connections, but the language is not primarily Void-associated |
| Harsh consonants | Low (ethereal names are "soft") | High (*N'raqi*, *K'thir*) | Ethereal phonology is distinctly softer than Shath'Yar |

## 12. Relationship to Other Languages

### Ethereal — Nerubian Connection

No confirmed genetic relationship. Both use apostrophe-marked morpheme boundaries, but this is a widespread WoW convention. The *Az-* element in *Azir* (ethereal) and *Azj-* (nerubian) is likely coincidental — the two species have no documented contact or shared ancestry.

### Ethereal — Shath'Yar Connection

Some ethereals (the Shadowguard) have been corrupted by the Void, and Dimensius (a Void Lord) destroyed K'aresh. This could imply some Shath'Yar influence on the ethereal language through Void contact:
- The apostrophe convention may be influenced by Shath'Yar orthography
- Void-corrupted ethereals may use harsher phonological patterns (not clearly attested)

**Confidence: Low** for direct Shath'Yar loanwords; **Moderate** for Void phonological influence on the Shadowguard dialect.

### Ethereal — Draenei Connection

Ethereals and Draenei both originate from worlds destroyed by the Burning Legion / Void (K'aresh and Argus respectively). Both species are refugees. However, no linguistic connection is confirmed:
- *K'aresh* and *Argus* share no morphological elements
- Ethereal names (*Salhadaar*, *Ve'nari*) and Draenei names (*Velen*, *Maraad*) have different phonological profiles
- Both use apostrophes, but this is a widespread WoW convention

### Ethereal Language Family: Hypothetical

```
K'aresh Language (unattested; native language of pre- destruction K'aresh)
├── Ethereal dialect (spoken by energy-transformed ethereals)
│   ├── Netherstorm variant (Consortium, Ethereum — TBC)
│   └── K'aresh variant (Shadowguard, Wastelanders — TWW)
└── Broker dialect (spoken by brokers)
    ├── Tazavesh variant (Shadowlands)
    └── K'aresh variant (K'aresh Trust — TWW)
```

This is entirely hypothetical. The relationship between ethereal and broker speech is supported by the shared *-esh* element and shared orthographic conventions, but no confirmed vocabulary overlap beyond place names has been established.

## 13. Writing System

Ethereals and brokers are known to have writing systems. In TBC, ethereal script appears on various objects in Netherstorm and Mana-Tombs. In Shadowlands, broker writing is visible in Tazavesh. The scripts appear to be glyphic or arcane in nature, consistent with the ethereals' energy-based physiology.

No ethereal or broker hieroglyphs or texts have been translated in canon.
