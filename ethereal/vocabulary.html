The *-esh* / *-aresh* Suffix: A Potential Locative

If -esh = "world" / "realm" / "home":
  • *K'aresh* = "[K'ar]-realm" or "[K']-home"
  • *Tazavesh* = "[Tazav]-realm" or "[Tazav]-home"
The *-aresh* form in *K'aresh* may be an extended variant with a linking vowel: *-a-resh*. The artifact name *Reshii* also contains the *Resh-* element, possibly the full root form of the *-esh* suffix (see §Reshii Investigation below).

Confidence: Moderate–High (upgraded based on *Reshii* plural analysis). Two place-name data points with consistent semantics, plus the *Reshii* artifact name providing a confirmed plural root form (multiple Ribbons existed, each held by a different Ravel member).

Investigation: *Reshii* and the *-esh* / *-resh* Root

The artifact name Reshii Ribbons contains the element Resh-, which may be the full root form of the -esh locative suffix. Wikipedia confirms that multiple Reshii Ribbons existed, each possessed by a different member of the Ravel — Locus-Walker had one, Ve'nari had one, a dead Ravel member had one (recovered from the Wastelanders), and Azir had the final one. The party had to collect all of them, and the Ravel previously used the "combined power of the Reshii Ribbons" to destroy K'aresh. This confirms *-ii* is a plural suffix and *Resh-* is the root noun meaning "realm/world."

The *resh* ~ *-esh* ~ *-aresh* Alternation

If *Resh-* is the full root form meaning "realm/world":
  • *Resh-* + *-ii* → *Reshii* = "realms" or "of the realm" (full root form)
  • *-esh* in *Tazavesh* = reduced form of *resh-* with /r/ loss after consonant cluster
  • *-aresh* in *K'aresh* = *-a-* + *resh* = "realm" with linking vowel
The /r/ loss in *-esh* would be conditioned by post-consonant position, a common sound change cross-linguistically.

Confidence: Moderate. Phonologically and semantically coherent; the plural confirmation (multiple Reshii Ribbons existed) strengthens the *-ii* suffix and *Resh-* root analysis.

Arguments Against

  • Only three data points; the phonological similarity could be coincidental
  • The /r/ loss is unidirectional and not confirmed by other data
  • The Ravel connection: *Reshii* Ribbons are associated with the Ravel, whose English name means "tangle/unravel" — *Resh-* could be related to this concept instead

Cross-Reference: Apostrophe-Marked Compounds Across "Alien" Languages

Four WoW languages share apostrophe-marked morpheme boundaries. This section analyzes whether the convention reflects genuine linguistic structure, Blizzard design aesthetics, or in-universe areal influence.

Apostrophe Patterns

LanguagePatternExamplesFrequency
Shath'YarInitial C' (prefix boundary)*N'Zoth*, *C'Thun*, *K'thir*Very high
NerubianMedial CVC' (compound boundary)*Anub'azal*, *Ovi'nax*, *Krix'vizk*Very high
QirajiMedial CVC' (compound boundary)*Ahn'qiraj*Moderate
EtherealBoth initial and medial*K'aresh*, *Ve'nari*Low
Key finding: Shath'Yar uses apostrophes to mark prefix boundaries (C' + root), while nerubian/qiraji/ethereal use them for compound boundaries (root + root). This structural difference suggests the convention is not a single shared feature but may have different origins in each language family.

Old God transmission chain (plausible): Shath'Yar → Aqir (Old God creation) → Nerubian + Qiraji (Aqir descendants). The apostrophe convention shifted from prefix-marking to compound-marking during Aqir development.

Ethereal position: Ethereals are NOT Old God creations (native to K'aresh). Their apostrophe usage is most likely either independent development or Blizzard aesthetic choice, not evidence of in-universe linguistic contact with Aqir-descended species.

Confidence: Low–Moderate for the Aqir-inheritance chain; High for Blizzard design aesthetic as at least a contributing factor.
