The *-esh* / *-aresh* Suffix: A Potential Locative

If -esh = "world" / "realm" / "home":
  • *K'aresh* = "[K'ar]-realm" or "[K']-home"
  • *Tazavesh* = "[Tazav]-realm" or "[Tazav]-home"
The *-aresh* form in *K'aresh* may be an extended variant with a linking vowel: *-a-resh*. The artifact name *Reshii* also contains the *Resh-* element, possibly related.

Confidence: Low–Moderate. Two place-name data points with consistent semantics, but coincidence cannot be excluded.
