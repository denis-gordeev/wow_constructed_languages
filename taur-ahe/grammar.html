Taur-ahe Grammar

Phonology

  • The language uses apostrophes to mark morpheme boundaries (inferred) from An'she, Mu'sha, Apa'ro
  • Common syllable pattern: CV'CV or CVC'V (inferred)
  • Vowel-heavy with long vowel sounds likely (inferred from the lilting quality described in lore)

Morphology

Compounding via Apostrophe

Apostrophes appear to join two meaningful elements into a single concept (inferred):

Prefix "An-"

  • "An" may function as a definite article or celestial honorific prefix (inferred)
  • Appears only in An'she; too few examples to confirm (uncertain)

Suffix "-ahe"

  • Appears in "Taur-ahe" and may denote "speech," "language," or "tongue" (inferred)
  • No other attested examples to confirm (uncertain)

Suffix "-a"

  • May denote a plural or collective noun (inferred) from "Taura" (people)
  • Alternatively a stem form without suffix (uncertain)

Syntax

  • No attested sentence structure; word order cannot be determined
  • The spiritual and nature-connected quality of the language is described in lore but has no grammatical attestation

Limitations

The grammar of Taur-ahe is almost entirely unreconstructed. Only a handful of noun phrases are attested, and no full sentences survive in canon. All rules above are speculative.
