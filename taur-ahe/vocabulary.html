Notes

  • IPA pronunciation for “Taur-ahe”: /tɔɹ.ˈɑːhɛh/ (TOR AH-heh), per the warcraft.wiki.gg article
  • Also spelled “Taurahe” and (non-canon TCG) “Taur’ahe”
  • Camp Taurajo is pronounced /tɔːrəhoʊ/ (as in Navajo), reflecting Amerindian language influence on Taur-ahe naming
  • Taur-ahe is at least partially written using pictograms (The Writ of History)
  • There are many different forms and dialects of Taur-ahe, differing based on tribe and location (Traveler, pg. 54)
  • Some algorithmic words from the in-game language parser (e.g., Echeyakee, Isha Awak, Lakota’mani, Ishamuhale, Owatanka, Washte Pawne, Lar’korwi, Lo’Gosh) have been made official canon; other algorithmic words are not canon
