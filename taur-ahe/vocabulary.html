Note on Taur-ahe sources: Taur-ahe has very limited attestation in canon. Most vocabulary derives from the tauren creation myth recounting the relationship of An'she (the Sun) and Mu'sha (the Moon), as well as proper names of notable tauren figures. The morphemic structure of Taur-ahe remains largely speculative; reconstructions are based on compound analysis (e.g., Taur-ahe = "earth" + "speech") but should be treated as uncertain.
