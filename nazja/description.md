# Nazja — Description

## Speakers

Nazja is the language of the Naga, the serpentine race that inhabits the ocean depths of Azeroth. It is spoken primarily by the naga of the Nazjatar Empire, ruled by Queen Azshara from the underwater capital of Nazjatar beneath the Maelstrom. The naga were formerly Highborne night elves (Quel'dorei) who were transformed by the Old God N'Zoth during the Great Sundering over 10,000 years ago.

Most naga speak Nazja as their primary language. Many also know Common, and some learn Darnassian — the language of their heritage — in order to communicate with their night elf cousins.

## History

Nazja is derived from Darnassian, the ancestral language of the night elves. Like Thalassian (the language of the High Elves / Blood Elves), Nazja diverged from Darnassian approximately 10,000 years ago when the Highborne were cast into the ocean depths during the Great Sundering.

The transformation from Highborne to naga fundamentally altered the speakers' vocal apparatus. Over ten millennia of isolation beneath the sea, Nazja evolved as its own unique language. Although it shares common heritage with Darnassian, the meanings and definitions of terms have diverged significantly. The naga's underwater environment and mutated physiology likely contributed to phonological shifts from the parent language.

The Warcraft RPG notes: "Nazja has evolved as its own unique language over 10,000 years, and has diverged from the Darnassian tongue. Although Nazja shares common heritage from the Darnassian tongue, over the course of its linguistic evolution the meanings and definitions of terms the language are no longer the same." (Monster Guide, pg. 106)

A fan theory (noted on the wiki as speculation) suggests that Nazja may not be a separate language at all, but rather Darnassian spoken underwater with a different vocal organ — which would account for the naga accent when speaking to surface dwellers and their ability to communicate with elves without difficulty. This remains unconfirmed.

## Cultural Context

- Naga society is deeply matriarchal. Female naga retain more of their elven features and intelligence; they serve as generals, magi, and leaders. Male naga are physically powerful but considered less intelligent; they serve as warriors and guards.
- Queen Azshara is venerated as a living demigod. In naga minds, Azshara, Nazjatar, and the naga themselves are inextricably linked — "perhaps the same thing, in a way."
- The naga have abandoned the deities of their kaldorei heritage (Malorne, Cenarius, Elune), who are now "nothing but memories" to them.
- Naga military organization deploys male infantry with tridents at moderate range while females cast spells from behind.
- The Naga Myrmidon and Royal Guard are elite male melee fighters; Sirens and Sea Witches are powerful female spellcasters.

## Relationship to Other Languages

- **Darnassian**: Direct ancestor language. Nazja is explicitly stated to be derived from Darnassian (Warcraft Encyclopedia). The shared morpheme *-dorei* ("children of / people of") appears in both languages. The compound pattern *Zin-Azshari* ("Glory of Azshara") is Darnassian, not Nazja, but demonstrates the shared compounding tradition.
- **Thalassian**: A parallel development from Darnassian. Both Nazja and Thalassian diverged from the same root under very different circumstances (Thalassian through the High Elves' exile to Quel'Thalas; Nazja through the Highborne's transformation and submersion).
- **Shalassian**: Another parallel development from Darnassian, spoken by the Nightborne of Suramar.
- The four elven dialects (Darnassian, Thalassian, Shalassian, Nazja) form a language family with confirmed shared morphological features, most notably the *-dorei* compound pattern and apostrophe-marked morpheme boundaries.
- Nazja's writing resembles elven script (Warcraft Encyclopedia). A treatise by Warlord Kalithresh was written in "the water-proofed alien script of the naga."

## Naga Naming Conventions

From the Warcraft RPG (Dark Factions, pg. 13):

Naga names have little in common. Some retain kaldorei traditions, taking surnames that speak to the strengths of their race. Others prefer more exotic names that may have some meaning in the naga tongue or in some other forgotten language. Still others adopt titles purely for their intimidation value, or to assert claims to leadership.

Their names often include many sibilants, Z's and V's, although this particular trait must have started to develop before the Highborne's transformation into naga, since names like Azshara, Zin-Azshari, Vashj, and Vashj'ir all date back to the time before the transformation, and there don't seem to be any similarly-sounding names in modern Darnassian.

- **Male Names**: Sakrasis, Shezril, Velrash, Zoniss
- **Female Names**: Anna, Asprah, Charib'dishal, Scilla, Ursula
- **Surnames**: Darkscale, Rendclaw, Serpens, Stormbow

## Reliability Notes

- **High confidence (canon)**: The name "Nazja" for the language; its derivation from Darnassian; the attested phrases from Warcraft III and WoW; the tribe/subtype names; the pronunciation of Nazjatar as "NAWZ-juh-tahr."
- **Moderate confidence (RPG, considered non-canon by Blizzard)**: The linguistic mechanics described in the RPG (Monster Guide, Dark Factions); the sample name lists; the spell incantations Gall'Mak and Gellin'Dallah; the assertion that Nazja has diverged enough from Darnassian to pose communication issues.
- **Speculative (wiki speculation)**: The sample word analysis (Dallah, Gall, Gellin, Mak); the theory that Nazja is Darnassian spoken with a different vocal apparatus; the derivation of "Nazjatar" from "Nazja" (or vice versa).
