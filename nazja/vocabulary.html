6. Phonological Observations

Key features of Nazja phonology:
  • Sibilant dominance — /s/, /z/, /ʃ/, /ʒ/ far exceed other elven languages
  • /ʒ/ (zh) is a distinctive Nazja sound, rare in Darnassian and Thalassian
  • /dʒ/ (j) very common: Naz'jar, Zin'jatar, Vashj, Sashj'tar
  • Vowel preference: /a/ and /e/ dominate; /o/ and /u/ are rare
  • Consonant clusters more complex than Darnassian: /ssz/, /sr/, /θr/
  • Apostrophe marks morpheme boundaries (inherited from elven tradition)

7. Relationship to Darnassian and Other Elven Languages

Elven Language Family

Proto-Elven (unattested)
└── Darnassian (Night Elf language; the ancestor)
    ├── Thalassian (High Elf / Blood Elf language)
    ├── Shalassian (Nightborne language)
    └── Nazja (Naga language)

All three daughter languages diverged from Darnassian ~10,000 years ago at the Great Sundering.

Key Divergence Factors

  • Physiological change — naga vocal apparatus fundamentally different from night elves
  • Underwater environment — 10,000 years of deep-sea isolation
  • Cultural revolution — abandonment of Elune/Cenarius; worship of Azshara and Old Gods
  • Old God influence — possible Shath'Yar loanwords or phonological influence (speculative)
  • Mutual intelligibility lost — RPG states Nazja has diverged enough to require study to communicate with Darnassian speakers
