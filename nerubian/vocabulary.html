The Azj- / Nazj- Connection

Key finding: The prefix Azj- in nerubian place names and Nazj- in naga place names may reflect a shared Aqir substrate morpheme.
NerubianNagaShared Element

If Nazj- = N- + Azj-, then the naga form adds an initial /n/ prefix to the same root. This could reflect an Aqir morpheme meaning "deep" or "realm," preserved in both descendant languages after 10,000+ years of divergence. Confidence: Low–Medium.
