Drust Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon (NPC dialogue, quest text, official Blizzard sources). Green = inferred morpheme; Gray italic = uncertain meaning. The Drust language has no attested translated phrases; all linguistic analysis is based on proper names only.

Attestation warning: The Drust language is extremely poorly attested. No complete Drust-language phrases with translations exist in canon. The entire corpus consists of approximately 7 Drust-language proper names (Gorak Tul, Gorak Zhar, Gol Osigr, Gol Inath, Gol Koval, Gol Var, Thros) plus names that may be Vrykul-retained rather than specifically Drust (Ulfar, Sef Iwen, Kivarr). All morpheme decompositions are speculative.

Key Structural Divergence: Prefix vs. Suffix

The most significant difference between Drust and mainland Vrykul: Drust uses the Gol- prefix for place names, whereas Vrykul uses suffixes (-heim, -garde, -bron, -korn). This prefix-based naming is more reminiscent of Titan facility naming (Uld- prefix: Ulduar, Uldum, Uldaman) than of mainland Vrykul. If the Gol- prefix is a Drust innovation that parallels Titan naming conventions, this could suggest that the Drust language preserved or reinvented a more Titan-like naming structure, possibly because the Drust maintained a closer connection to their titan-forged origins through druidism.
