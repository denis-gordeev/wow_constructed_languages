# Gnomish Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon sources (in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard publications). No invented words are included. IPA transcriptions are approximate, based on the romanized spelling.

**Note on attestation:** The Gnomish language proper has extremely low attestation. Gnomes speak Common in-game, and their native language is mentioned but rarely heard. Most entries below are proper names of Gnomish origin that may preserve Gnomish-language morphemes. The Mechagnome connection (titan-forged progenitors) provides the strongest link to the Titan language family.

### Analysis: Goblin Naming Patterns

Goblin names in the Undermine campaign reinforce existing patterns:

1. **Short punchy given names**: *Pamsy*, *Nikki*, *Marin* — CVCV or CVC patterns, consistent with goblin cultural emphasis on speed and efficiency.

2. **Compound surnames**: *Fizzlecrank*, *Noggenfogger* — Common-language descriptive compounds reflecting goblin engineering culture.

3. **Potential *-gaz* / *Gaz-* morpheme**: *Gazlowe*, *Revilgaz* both contain *Gaz-* / *-gaz*. If this is a genuine goblin morpheme, it could mean "trade" or "value" — central to goblin culture. **Confidence: Very Low** — only two attestations.

4. **Potential *-wix* / *-ik* name suffix**: *Gallywix*, *Renzik* share similar final-syllable patterns (*-ɪks* and *-ɪk*). If *-ik* / *-iks* is a goblin name suffix, it may mark personal names. **Confidence: Very Low** — only two attestations.

5. **Gallagio *-gio* ending**: The *-gio* / *-agio* ending in *Gallagio* is unique. If it reflects a goblin/Gnomish place-name suffix (paralleling *-agon* in *Mechagon*), it would be the first attestation of a goblin place-name morpheme. **Confidence: Very Low** — single attestation; may simply be an Italian-style aesthetic choice by Blizzard.

## Relationship to Other Languages

### Titan (Progenitor Language)

Gnomes are descended from Mechagnomes, who were created by Keeper Mimiron using the Forge of Wills. The Mechagnomes are a titan-forged race, making Gnomish a descendant of Titan (via Mechagnome). Key connections:

- *Mech-* / *Mekka-* may be a Titan root preserved in Gnomish, related to mechanical creation and the name of Keeper Mimiron.
- The structural similarity between Gnomish *-regan* (in *Gnomeregan*) and Titan facility suffixes (*-uar*, *-um*, *-aman*) is too weak to establish a sound correspondence.
- Gnomes retained knowledge of Titan technology (Gnomeregan was built over Titan ruins) and may preserve Titan vocabulary for technical concepts.

### Dwarven (Fellow Titan-Forged Descendant)

Gnomes and Dwarves have coexisted in Ironforge for centuries. Their languages share a common ancestor (Titan) but diverged through different progenitors (Mechagnomes vs. Earthen). Shared features may include:

- Right-headed compounding (inherited from Titan)
- Place-naming conventions
- Shared Titan vocabulary for earth/crafting concepts

### Common (Trade Language)

Gnomes speak Common as their primary language. Most gnomish names and technological terminology in WoW is rendered in Common. The Gnomish language proper is mentioned but rarely heard or seen in-game.

### Vrykul (Remote Cognate)

Gnomes and Vrykul are distant cousins through the titan-forged lineage (Mechagnomes vs. Iron Vrykul). No direct linguistic connection is attested beyond the shared Titan ancestor.

## Egyptian/Real-World Parallels

Unlike Tol'vir (Egyptian) or Vrykul (Norse), Gnomish does not show strong real-world linguistic inspiration. Gnomish names in WoW tend toward:

- English compound surnames (Featherwhistle, Brassbolts, Steelspark)
- Puns and wordplay (Evol = love reversed, Gnomercy = no mercy)
- Technical vocabulary (Spark, Bolt, Gear)

This naming convention is consistent with the gnomish cultural emphasis on humor, invention, and accessibility rather than ancient tradition.
