Titan Connections

Gnomish as a Titan-forged descendant: Gnomes descend from Mechagnomes, who were created by Keeper Mimiron using the Forge of Wills. This makes Gnomish a daughter language of Titan, parallel to how Dwarven descends from Titan via the Earthen lineage. The *Mech-* / *Mekka-* root may be a Titan morpheme preserved in Gnomish, related to mechanical creation and the name of Keeper Mimiron. The structural features of Gnomish (right-headed compounding, initial stress, preference for complex onsets) are consistent with other Titan-forged languages.

The Gnomish language has been heavily influenced by Common through centuries of bilingualism, making it difficult to identify genuine Gnomish morphemes in the extant corpus. The mechagnome dialect of Mechagon may preserve older, more Titan-influenced vocabulary.
