The -dar Suffix: Earthen or Arathi?

Assessment: The -dar suffix in Beledar and Mereldar most likely derives from Titan -dor (land/realm) via Earthen. The suffix appears only in Khaz Algar — not in any other Arathi or Common place names — which strongly suggests an Earthen source (substrate or contact borrowing) rather than independent Arathi innovation. Confidence: Low–Medium.

Arathi Personal Names (Hallowfall)

The Arathi of Hallowfall are a mixed human–high-elf population. Their personal names reflect this dual heritage, though the sample is too small for morphological analysis.

The primary linguistic significance of the Arathi data is the -dar toponymic suffix in place names (*Beledar*, *Mereldar*), which connects to the Earthen/Titan *-dor* morpheme and suggests that the Arathi adopted Earthen place names when they settled in Hallowfall.
