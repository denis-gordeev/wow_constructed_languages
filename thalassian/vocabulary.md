# Thalassian Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard sources. No invented words are included.

## Nouns

| Word | Translation | Source / Notes |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Sin | Blood | Sin'dorei ("Children of the Blood") |
| Quel | High, Noble | Quel'dorei ("Children of Noble Birth"), Quel'Thalas ("High Home") |
| Ren | Void | Ren'dorei ("Children of the Void") |
| Belore | Sun | Anar'alah belore ("By the light of the sun"); also appears in Blood Elf names |
| Alah | Light | Anar'alah — alah = "light" |
| Thalas | Home, Kingdom | Quel'Thalas ("High Home" / "High Kingdom") |
| Thoras | Troubles, Burdens | Ande'thoras-ethil ("May your troubles be diminished") |
| Anar | By (preposition); possibly also "light" | Anar'alah belore — anar = "by" |
| Malanorei | Travelers, Wanderers | Bal'a dash, malanorei ("Greetings, traveler") |

## Adjectives / Verbs

| Word | Translation | Source / Notes |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Ethil | Diminished, Lessened | Ande'thoras-ethil ("May your troubles be diminished") |
| Dela'na | Will guide us | Anu belore dela'na ("The sun will guide us") |
| Asha | Blessed, Grace | Uncertain; limited attestation |

## Particles / Grammatical Markers

| Word | Translation | Source / Notes |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Anu | The (definite article or subject marker) | Anu belore dela'na ("The sun will guide us") |
| Ande | May (optative marker) | Ande'thoras-ethil ("May your troubles be diminished") |
| Dorei | Children of, People of | Sin'dorei, Quel'dorei, Ren'dorei, Kaldorei — productive suffix |
| Dei | Of (genitive/relational particle) | Appears within dorei as -'dorei; -dei = "of" |

## Proper Names

| Name | Translation | Source / Notes |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Sin'dorei | Children of the Blood | Blood Elves' self-designation |
| Quel'dorei | Children of Noble Birth / Highborne | High Elves / Highborne self-designation |
| Ren'dorei | Children of the Void | Void Elves' self-designation |
| Kaldorei | Children of the Stars | Night Elves' self-designation (shared with Darnassian) |
| Quel'Thalas | High Home / High Kingdom | Kingdom of the High Elves / Blood Elves |
| Medivh | Keeper of Secrets | Title / proper name |
| Dath'Remar | (Proper name) | First of the Highborne / High Elves |
| El'darath | (Place name) | Attested location |
| Thalas'darah | Silvermoon? | Uncertain; possibly "Silvermoon" but not firmly attested |
| Antenorian | Governor of Tranquillien (Midnight) | Allied with Twilight's Blade cultists. No clear Thalassian morpheme decomposition; possibly a personal name with *-orian* ending |
| Silversun Compact | Faction (Midnight) | Alliance of Sunreavers and Silver Covenant formed by Aethas Sunreaver and Vereesa Windrunner after the Battle of the Darkwell. Portmanteau of "Silver" (Silver Covenant) + "Sun" (Sunreavers; cf. *belore* = "sun"). Common-language compound, not Thalassian, but culturally significant — the "sun" element echoes *belore*, the Thalassian word for "sun" |
