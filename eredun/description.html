Eredun / Demonic Language — Description

Speakers

Eredun is the language of the Eredar, the ancient and powerful race from the planet Argus. After Sargeras corrupted the Eredar, those who joined the Burning Legion became known as Man’ari (“corrupt” / “unnatural” in Draenei), while those who fled under Velen retained the name Draenei (“exiled ones”). The Eredar who became demons continued speaking Eredun, which became associated with demonic magic and the Burning Legion.

The term “Demonic” is sometimes used interchangeably with Eredun, but properly, Eredun refers to the original Eredar language, while “Demonic” may encompass a broader set of languages used by the Burning Legion’s diverse races (including Eredun, Shath’Yar influences, and other demonic tongues).

Primary speakers:

  • The Man’ari Eredar: Archimonde, Kil’jaeden, and their followers — the primary speakers of the corrupted form of Eredun.
  • Draenei: The uncorrupted Eredar retain knowledge of Eredun but primarily speak Draenei (their dialect of the same language). See Draenei vocabulary.
  • Demon recruits: Other races corrupted by the Burning Legion may learn Eredun/Demonic as a lingua franca of the Legion.
  • Warlocks: Mortal practitioners of fel magic study Eredun/Demonic for spellcasting and summoning rituals.

History

Eredun predates the Burning Legion. It was the native language of the Eredar on Argus, a civilization of immense magical and intellectual achievement. When Sargeras offered the Eredar power in exchange for service, the race split:

  • Man’ari Eredar (Archimonde, Kil’jaeden) joined the Legion and their language became infused with fel energy — warpcantations, binding words, and summoning formulae.
  • Draenei (Velen’s followers) fled Argus and eventually settled on Draenor. Their dialect diverged into what is now called Draenei (see Draenei).

Eredun is one of the oldest attested languages in WoW lore, potentially predating the Titan-ordered Azerothian languages by millennia.

Relationship to Draenei

Eredun and Draenei are the same language with cultural and lexical divergence. The Draenei fled Argus approximately 25,000 years before the First War. After 25 millennia of separation:

  • Phonological divergence: Likely significant, though the small corpus makes comparison difficult.
  • Lexical divergence: The Man’ari developed vocabulary for fel magic, soul binding, and demonic pacts. The Draenei developed vocabulary for the Naaru, the Light, and exile.
  • Shared roots: The core morphological pattern (draen = exile, or = world, ei = people) is shared, confirming common origin.
  • Man’ari is itself a Draenei/Eredun word meaning “wrong / unnatural / corrupt” — the Draenei apply it to their corrupted kin.

Cultural Context

  • Eredun is associated with fel magic, demonic pacts, and the Burning Legion. Warlocks study it for summoning and binding rituals.
  • The Man’ari Eredar are among the most powerful beings in WoW lore; their language reflects their arcane and military sophistication.
  • Eredun incantations appear in-game as “Demonic” script, but this script is decorative and does not map to a real phonological system.
  • The apostrophe convention (Man’ari, Kil’jaeden, Mac’Aree) marks morpheme boundaries, consistent with other WoW language conventions.

Attestation and Reliability

Low to Moderate. More attested than many WoW languages due to:

  • Numerous demon and Eredar names in lore
  • Warlock spell incantations (though these are often in “Demonic” script that doesn’t map to real phonology)
  • Draenei vocabulary that provides cognates
  • The word Man’ari and its cultural significance
However, no complete Eredun sentences are attested outside of spell incantations and proper names.

What is reliable

  • Man’ari = “wrong/corrupt/unnatural” is confirmed by in-game usage.
  • The roots draen (“exile/refuge”), ei (“ones/people”), and or (“world/land”) are well-supported by Blizzard’s official translations of Draenei and Draenor.
  • Sha’tar = “Born of Light” and Shattrath = “Dwelling of Light” are confirmed by Blizzard.

What is uncertain

  • The meaning of individual demon names (Archimonde, Kil’jaeden, Mannoroth, etc.) — these may contain Eredun morphemes or belong to other demonic languages.
  • Whether Mac’Aree carries a meaning in Eredun (possibly “beloved” or “sanctuary”).
  • The phonological analysis, which is based solely on English-language orthography.

What is unknown

  • Syntax (word order, case marking, agreement).
  • Verbal morphology.
  • Any vocabulary beyond proper names and the shared Draenei cognates.
