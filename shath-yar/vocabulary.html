Naaru Names: Cross-Reference with Shath'Yar Naming

The Naaru — beings of Holy Light — consistently use names that follow the Shath'Yar initial-apostrophe convention (consonant-apostrophe-vowel sequence). This is notable because Naaru are the antithesis of Old Gods and the Void, yet their names are morphologically indistinguishable from Shath'Yar names.

Lothraxion is a unique case: a Light-infused being serving as "high commander of the Vanguard" in Midnight. His name lacks the apostrophe convention entirely and follows a Latinate morphological pattern (*Loth-* + *-raxion*), more similar to Eredun/Draenei naming than to Naaru or Shath'Yar. He may not be a Naaru but a Light-infused being of another race.

Interpretation: Three possibilities: (1) Naaru names are genuinely Shath'Yar — the naming convention predates the Light-Void split; (2) Naaru names predate Shath'Yar — the apostrophe convention is a universal cosmic naming pattern; (3) Blizzard design convergence — the apostrophe is an aesthetic choice for "otherworldly" names applied to both Light and Void entities.

Confidence: Low–Medium that Naaru names reflect genuine Shath'Yar naming conventions rather than design convergence. The systematic parallel (all Naaru use the convention) is stronger than a coincidence would predict, but the Light-Void opposition makes in-universe transmission unlikely unless the naming convention predates the split.

Investigation: *domanaar* and the *-aar* Suffix

The *-aar* ending in *domanaar* parallels the ethereal *-daar* suffix in *Salhadaar* (Nexus-King). Three hypotheses:

  • Ethereal loanword: *-daar* → *-aar* (loss of /d/) via Shadowguard contact with Xal'atath's Void forces. Plausible transmission mechanism but undermined by possibility that *-daar* is itself an Arabic design artifact. Confidence: Very Low–Low
  • Shared Void nomenclature: *-aar* is an independent Void/Shath'Yar morpheme that coincidentally resembles *-daar*. Confidence: Medium
  • Blizzard design convergence: Both independently reflect Blizzard's aesthetic for "authoritative" name endings. Weakened by different design traditions (Arabic for ethereal, Latin for domanaar names). Confidence: Low
Note on Old God whispers: Many of N'Zoth's and Yogg-Saron's in-game whispers are delivered in English (Common) rather than Shath'Yar proper. The Shath'Yar phrases (e.g., Ywaq maq oou) are rare and appear primarily in specific encounter dialogue and item flavor text. The English-language whispers are included here for completeness as they are the primary way Old Gods communicate in canon. N'Zoth's whispers from Battle for Azeroth (patch 8.3) and the N'Zoth puzzle box are among the most extensively attested examples.
