# Shath'Yar — Description

## Speakers

Shath'Yar is the language of the Old Gods and their servants. It is spoken by the Old Gods themselves (Yogg-Saron, N'Zoth, C'Thun, Y'Shaarj), their faceless servants (N'raqi, K'thir), and likely the Aqir and Qiraji in some form. Mortals who hear Shath'Yar are often driven mad by it.

## History

Shath'Yar predates all mortal languages on Azeroth. The Old Gods were among the first beings on the planet, having been implanted by the Void Lords. Their language is thus among the oldest in existence, though it has no evolutionary relationship to any mortal tongue.

The language was first encountered by players in the whispers of Yogg-Saron in Ulduar (Wrath of the Lich King) and later expanded through N'Zoth's dialogue and the Ny'alotha raid (Battle for Azeroth). The Qiraji in Ahn'Qiraj (vanilla WoW) also represent speakers of a related tongue.

## Cultural Context

- Shath'Yar is described as maddening and whispering. Hearing it causes mental deterioration in mortals.
- The language is tied to the Void and the Old Gods' desire to corrupt and consume.
- Whispers are a primary vector of Old God influence; the language itself is a weapon.
- The faceless ones (N'raqi) serve as priests and commanders, suggesting a liturgical or command-function for the language.
- The insectoid servants (Aqir, Qiraji, possibly Ssra'thik) may speak a related dialect.

## Relationship to Other Languages

- Shath'Yar may be related to the Void language spoken by the Void ethereals and other Shadow-aligned beings, but this is unconfirmed.
- The language shares the apostrophe-heavy phonology with some Eredun (Demonic) words, possibly due to the Burning Legion's historical interaction with the Old Gods, but no genetic relationship is established.
- N'raqi, K'thir, and Qiraji speech may represent dialects or registers of Shath'Yar rather than separate languages.

## Reliability Notes

- **Very limited attestation.** Proper names and a few untranslated phrases are all that survive.
- The untranslated phrases ("Ywaq maq oou," "Iilth vwah, uhn'agth fhssh za") have no confirmed translations and may be pure gibberish designed for atmosphere.
- Most "Old God dialogue" in the game is rendered in English for player comprehension, not in Shath'Yar.
- The language was likely designed for aesthetic effect (alien, maddening) rather than as a functional constructed language.
- Any morphological analysis (suffixes, prefixes) is highly speculative.
- The association of N'raqi, Aqir, Qiraji, and K'thir with Shath'Yar is inferred from their service to the Old Gods, not explicitly stated in canon.
