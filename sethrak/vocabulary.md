# Sethrak Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon sources (in-game dialogue, NPC speech, quest text, or official Blizzard publications). No invented words are included.

**Warning:** The Sethrak language has extremely low attestation. No glossed words, phrases, or sentences exist beyond proper names. All glosses below are speculative unless otherwise noted.
