Dialectal Variation

  • Lore states that elementals speak "the language of their respective plane" (inferred)
  • This may mean Kalimag has four major dialects corresponding to the four elemental planes (inferred)

Limitations

Kalimag grammar is entirely unattested. No sentences, verb forms, or grammatical particles are known. The language exists only as a name and a set of proper nouns. All morphological analysis is speculative.
