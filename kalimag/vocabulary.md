## Frostmourne Inscription

The Kalimag runes on Frostmourne's pedestal in Frostmourne Cavern translate to: "Whosoever takes up this blade shall wield power eternal. Just as the blade rends flesh, so must power scar the spirit." (confirmed — *Arthas: Rise of the Lich King*, chapter 15). The runes were protected by the Guardian and revenants. Muradin Bronzebeard could read Kalimag; Arthas Menethil could not.

## Draenor Kalimag

The Furies of Draenor have their own version of Kalimag. The Burning Blade clan uses a runic language similar to the Ignan dialect spoken by Azeroth's fire elementals to bend elemental power to their will (confirmed — Flamebender's Tome quest). This suggests Kalimag exists in variant forms across worlds.
