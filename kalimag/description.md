# Kalimag — Description

## Speakers

Kalimag is the language of the elementals — the beings native to the Elemental Plane. It is spoken by fire, water, earth, and air elementals, as well as by their lords: Ragnaros (Fire), Al'Akir (Air), Neptulon (Water), and Therazane (Earth). Shamans may have some understanding of Kalimag through their communion with elemental spirits.

## History

The Elemental Plane was created by the Titan Keepers to imprison the elementals who ravaged Azeroth during the primordial age. Kalimag likely predates mortal languages, though it may have evolved during the long imprisonment of the elementals. The language is ancient and primal, reflecting the raw forces it represents.

The Shaman class draws on Kalimag through their connection to the elements. The word "Shaman" itself may derive from Kalimag, meaning "one who speaks with spirits," though this etymology is uncertain and "shaman" may instead originate from the orcish language.

## Cultural Context

- Kalimag is described as primal and forceful, reflecting the raw power of the elements.
- Each elemental plane may have its own dialect or register of Kalimag (inferred from lore stating elementals speak "the language of their respective plane").
- Fire elementals may speak in a harsher, more aggressive register; water elementals in a flowing register; etc. (inferred from elemental characterization)
- Shamans serve as intermediaries between mortal races and elementals, suggesting Kalimag is not easily learned by non-elementals.
- The language is tied to the fundamental forces of the world — fire, water, earth, and air.

## Relationship to Other Languages

- Kalimag may share roots with the Titan language, since the Elemental Plane was created by the Titans, but this is unconfirmed.
- The word "Shaman" may be a Kalimag loanword into Orcish and Common, but this is uncertain.
- Kalimag has no confirmed relationship to any mortal language.

## Reliability Notes

- **Extremely limited attestation.** Only the language name and proper nouns of the Elemental Lords and a few other terms are known.
- No full sentences, verb forms, or grammatical structures are attested.
- "Cyruush" and "Fahss" appear in datamined or out-of-context sources and their meaning is entirely uncertain.
- "Rajh" is a Tol'vir name and may not be Kalimag at all.
- The elemental planes may each have distinct languages rather than dialects of Kalimag.
- Most in-game elemental dialogue is rendered in Common.
- Any morphological analysis is highly speculative.
