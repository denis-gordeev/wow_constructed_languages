# Kalimag Grammar

## Phonology

- Described as a primal, forceful language
- The language likely varies by elemental plane — fire, water, earth, and air elementals may have distinct phonological registers (inferred from lore)
- Apostrophes appear in some names (Al'Akir) but not others (Ragnaros, Neptulon, Therazane), suggesting they mark specific morpheme boundaries rather than being a universal feature (inferred)
- Proper names show varied phonological patterns:
  - Heavy consonant clusters: Ragnaros, Therazane
  - Apostrophe-marked compounds: Al'Akir
  - Flowing syllables: Neptulon, Thunderaan

## Morphology

### Apostrophe Compounding

- **Al'Akir** = Al + Akir → possibly "lord" + "wind" or similar (uncertain)
- This parallels the compounding seen in Shath'Yar and Taur-ahe (inferred)

### Possible Name-Forming Suffixes

- **-os** appears in Ragnaros, (Neptulon has -on which may be related) — possibly a divine or lordly suffix (uncertain)
- **-an** appears in Thunderaan, Therazane — possibly an elemental name suffix (uncertain)
- These could also reflect different elemental phonological preferences rather than morphological markers (uncertain)

### Possible Elemental Lexical Fields

The names of the Elemental Lords may encode their elemental domain (inferred):

- **Ragnaros**: "Rag-" may relate to fire/raging (uncertain)
- **Al'Akir**: "Akir" may relate to wind/air (uncertain)
- **Neptulon**: "Nept-" may relate to water (uncertain, possibly a nod to Neptune)
- **Therazane**: "Ther-" may relate to earth (uncertain)

These are highly speculative etymologies and may simply be proper names without internal morphology.

## Syntax

- No sentence-level attestation exists
- Word order cannot be determined
- Elementals in-game speak in Common or their patron's language; no Kalimag sentences are recorded

## Dialectal Variation

- Lore states that elementals speak "the language of their respective plane" (inferred)
- This may mean Kalimag has four major dialects corresponding to the four elemental planes (inferred)
- Alternatively, each plane may have a distinct language, with "Kalimag" as a collective term (uncertain)

## Limitations

Kalimag grammar is entirely unattested. No sentences, verb forms, or grammatical particles are known. The language exists only as a name and a set of proper nouns. All morphological analysis is speculative.
