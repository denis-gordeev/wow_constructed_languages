Note on elemental domains: The four Elemental Planes — Firelands, Skywall, Abyssal Maw, and Deepholm — were created when the Titans banished the Elemental Lords from Azeroth. Kalimag itself is poorly documented, with most known words being proper names of powerful elementals.
