Gutterspeak Vocabulary

Important: Gutterspeak has virtually no uniquely attested vocabulary. The Forsaken were human citizens of Lordaeron who were raised as undead. They continue to speak Common with archaic, morbid, and culturally specific expressions, but no distinct lexicon has been recorded in canon. The entries below are English terms and proper names used within Forsaken culture. Where no meaningful phonological distinction exists, IPA is marked —.

All entries reflect terms used within Forsaken culture. Sienna = inferred or associated term; gray = uncertain; Light yellow = new additions.

Confirmed Lore

Gutterspeak is described as "a lower form of Common that uses little (very little) bits of Dwarven and Thalassian" (confirmed — World of Warcraft Battle Chest Guide). It evolved in the shady underground of black markets and rogues' guilds as the tongue spoken by people of ill-repute. When the Forsaken took the Undercity, they adopted Gutterspeak as their official language.

Notes

  • Gutterspeak has virtually no uniquely attested vocabulary. The Forsaken were human citizens of Lordaeron who were raised as undead.
  • The RPG (non-canon) states that Forsaken "have not lost the ability to understand Common, but just never choose to speak it under normal circumstances" (Horde Player's Guide).
  • In the WoW beta, Forsaken player characters spoke Common (besides Orcish) and could communicate with Alliance characters; this led to cross-faction harassment, so Gutterspeak was introduced as a game-mechanic language barrier.
  • The in-game language parser for Gutterspeak shares similar words with Common and Gnomish, reflecting their shared origin.
  • Since Legion, cross-faction communication has become increasingly possible (demon hunters speak Demonic, void elves speak Thalassian), but Gutterspeak/Common remains separated.
