Gutterspeak Vocabulary

Important: Gutterspeak has virtually no uniquely attested vocabulary. The Forsaken were human citizens of Lordaeron who were raised as undead. They continue to speak Common with archaic, morbid, and culturally specific expressions, but no distinct lexicon has been recorded in canon. The entries below are English terms and proper names used within Forsaken culture. IPA transcriptions reflect English/Common pronunciation, not a distinct Gutterspeak phonology. Where no meaningful phonological distinction exists, IPA is marked —.

Note on the nature of Gutterspeak: Unlike languages such as Orcish, Darnassian, or Eredun, Gutterspeak has no attested unique vocabulary, grammar, or phonology in Warcraft canon. The Forsaken were once human citizens of Lordaeron, and their "language" is essentially corrupted Common — archaic English flavored with morbid and death-obsessed expressions. The in-game language filter that prevents cross-faction communication is the primary evidence that Blizzard considers Gutterspeak distinct from Common, but no original lexicon has ever been published or developed. All terms above are English words and proper names that carry cultural significance within Forsaken society, not unique linguistic forms.
