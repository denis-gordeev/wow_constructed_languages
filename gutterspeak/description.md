# Gutterspeak — Description

## Speakers

Gutterspeak is the language of the Forsaken, the undead who broke free from the Lich King's control and established their own nation under Sylvanas Windrunner. It is spoken by the Forsaken of the Undercity and by undead across Azeroth. In practice, virtually all Forsaken also speak Common, and Gutterspeak is not a distinct language in any meaningful linguistic sense.

## History

The Forsaken were once human citizens of the kingdom of Lordaeron. When the Lich King's plague killed and raised them as undead, they retained their language — Common. When Sylvanas freed a portion of the Scourge and founded the Forsaken, their language remained Common with cultural modifications.

The name "Gutterspeak" is likely a pejorative applied by the living, or a self-mocking adoption by the Forsaken themselves. It reflects the perception that the Forsaken speak a degraded, corrupted form of Common.

Over the years since the Forsaken's founding, their speech has developed distinct cultural markers — morbid humor, archaic phrasing, and death-related metaphor — but no structural linguistic changes have been attested.

## Cultural Context

- Forsaken speech is characterized by dark humor, cynicism, and a preoccupation with death and decay (observed in NPC dialogue).
- The "Dark Lady" title for Sylvanas is the most prominent Forsaken-specific linguistic feature.
- Forsaken may use archaic or formal phrasing that reflects their origins as Lordaeron citizens (inferred).
- The Forsaken's isolation from the living may have led to some lexical drift, but this is not attested in canon.
- The Royal Apothecary Society and its work on the New Plague may have generated technical vocabulary, but none is recorded as uniquely Gutterspeak.
- The Forsaken's relationship with the Horde introduced them to Orcish and Trolls, but no borrowing is attested.

## Relationship to Other Languages

- **Common**: Gutterspeak is a register or dialect of Common. It shares all grammatical and most lexical features with Common.
- **Lordaeron Common**: The Forsaken's original dialect, likely a northern or formal register of Common.
- No other language relationships exist. The Forsaken are linguistically human.

## Reliability Notes

- **Gutterspeak is not a distinct language in any linguistic sense.** It is Common with cultural styling.
- No unique words, grammatical structures, or phonological features are attested for Gutterspeak in canon.
- The classification of Gutterspeak as a separate language is a game mechanic to allow Forsaken players to communicate privately.
- Any claims about unique Forsaken vocabulary or grammar are fan invention.
- The name "Gutterspeak" itself may not be used within the lore as an in-universe term; it appears primarily in game-mechanic contexts.
- The most reliable observation about Gutterspeak is that it does not exist as a separate language — it is Common, spoken by the dead.
