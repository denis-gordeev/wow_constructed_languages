## Notes

Gutterspeak has virtually no uniquely attested vocabulary. The Forsaken were human citizens of Lordaeron who were raised as undead. They continue to speak Common with archaic, morbid, and culturally specific expressions, but no distinct lexicon has been recorded in canon.
