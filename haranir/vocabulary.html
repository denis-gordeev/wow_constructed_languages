Hara'ni (Haranir) Vocabulary

All entries are attested in World of Warcraft canon sources. No invented words are included. Green = inferred morpheme; Gray italic = uncertain meaning. Bold CONFIRMED = translation confirmed in canon.

Attestation warning: The Hara'ni language has extremely low attestation. No Hara'ni-language sentences or phrases are attested in canon. All Haranir NPCs speak Common in-game. Most entries are proper names that may preserve Hara'ni morphemes. The language name Hara'ni is explicitly confirmed in the wiki infobox for the Haranir race. Six translations are now confirmed (see below).

Midnight Expansion: New Data (2026)

The World of Warcraft: Midnight expansion (released March 2, 2026) provides significant new Haranir lore and linguistic data. Orweyna, Hagar, and Ruia all reappear with expanded roles.

Key New Linguistic Data

  1. Ruutani — A race native to Harandar, affected by the Lightbloom. Both hostile and peaceful ruutani villages exist. The name *Ruutani* may be a Hara'ni word (see Beings for analysis). If genuine, it is the first non-Haranir proper name in the language.
  2. Alndust — A rare material that cures ruutani of the Lightbloom; "believed to have been blessed by the haranir's goddess." The second attestation of the *Aln-* morpheme (after *Aln'hara*), significantly strengthening the case for *Aln-* as a genuine Hara'ni root meaning "deep/sacred/blessed." See Cultural Names for full analysis.
  3. Haranir goddess — The Haranir goddess IS Aln'hara (the Azeroth Worldsoul), resolving the "unnamed goddess" question. The Haranir worship the nascent titan world-soul of Azeroth as their goddess. The Shul'ka sever their connection to Aln'hara's song to safely patrol the Rift of Aln.
  4. Harandar as playable zone — Harandar is a playable zone in Midnight, which may yield significantly more Haranir NPC names, dialogue, and place names from in-game content.
  5. Lightbloom — Plant life infused with the Light; first appeared in Harandar because of the Sunwell's influence. Common-language designation, not Hara'ni.
  6. Shul'ka initiation ritual — Initiate collects poisonous glands and "Healing Waters of Ahl'ua", finds a ritual site, consumes poison and water, fights for their life while unconscious; if they survive, they must slay Morta'ka the Sundered Echo as their final rite. Amarakk was mutated by this ritual.
  7. Vordrassil's roots — Kawayn confirms that Vordrassil's roots do NOT reach Harandar (they were severed due to corruption).
  8. Alpha build change — Alpha build of Midnight had a mural depicting Haranir as progenitors of all trollkind; this was changed at launch to depict a "split" in Haranir society.

Plot Events Affecting Haranir Lore

  • Ruia redirected Lightbloom to Eversong as revenge after the Sunwell caused Lightbloom to first appear in Harandar; stopped by Hagar

The *Aln-* Root

The *Aln-* root now has four attestations, making it one of the best-attested Hara'ni morphemes:

NameDecompositionProposed Gloss
*Aln'hara**Aln'* + *-hara*CONFIRMED: "The Worldsoul of Azeroth." If *-hara* = "world/soul" (confirmed via *Aln'hara*), then *Aln'* = "deep/primordial/essence" — the deep essence of the Worldsoul
*Alndust**Aln-* + *-dust*"Sacred dust" or "dust of the deep" — rare material blessed by the Haranir goddess
*Rift of Aln**Aln*Rift in Harandar/Emerald Dream. The name "Rift of Aln" uses *Aln* as a standalone element, not compounded — suggesting *Aln* can function as an independent noun
*Alnscorned**Aln-* + *scorned*Broken nightmares in the Rift of Aln. *-scorned* is a Common-language element appended to the Hara'ni *Aln-*

With the confirmed translation of *Aln'hara* as "The Worldsoul of Azeroth," we can now refine the gloss:

  • *Aln'* / *Aln-* = "deep/primordial/essence" — the deep, essential, or sacred quality of something
  • *Aln'hara* = "the deep soul" or "the essence of the world" → "The Worldsoul of Azeroth"
  • *Alndust* = "sacred dust" or "essence-dust" — dust imbued with the deep essence
  • *Rift of Aln* = "Rift of the Deep/Essence" — a tear in the deep fabric of the Emerald Dream
  • *Alnscorned* = "deep-scorned" or "essence-scorned" — nightmares broken by the deep essence

The absence of apostrophe in *Alndust* (vs. *Aln'hara*) may reflect phonological conditioning: the /d/ after *Aln* eliminates the need for a glottal stop marker, whereas *Aln'hara* needs one before the /h/. *Alnscorned* similarly lacks an apostrophe before /s/. *Rift of Aln* uses *Aln* as an independent noun, requiring no apostrophe. Alternatively, *Alndust* and *Alnscorned* may be Common-language compounds with a Hara'ni loan element.

Confidence: High for *Aln-* as a genuine Hara'ni morpheme (four attestations with semantically coherent glosses, one confirmed translation).

The *-dar* Toponymic Suffix

*Harandar* contains the suffix *-dar*, which also appears in Earthen (*Dornogal*) and Arathi (*Beledar*, *Mereldar*) toponyms within Khaz Algar.

If *-dar* = *-dor* (Titan "land/realm"), then *Harandar* = "Hara-land" — a toponym formed using a borrowed Titan-derived suffix. This would place Hara'ni toponymic conventions within the Khaz Algar areal sphere.

Confidence: Low–Moderate for *-dar* as a borrowed toponymic suffix. See earthen/vocabulary.html for cross-reference.

Phonological Observations

Sound Inventory

Consonants: ch (/tʃ/), d, dh (/ð/), g, h, k, l, m, n, ng (/ŋ/), r, s, t, th (/θ/), v, y (/j/), zh (/ʒ/), j (/dʒ/)

Vowels: a, e, i, o, u

Key Features

  • High /h/ frequency: 6+ of ~30+ names contain /h/ — unusually high for a WoW language
  • Medial apostrophe: Consistent compound-boundary marking (~25 of ~30+ names)
  • Geminate consonants: *Hannan* (-nn-), *Amarakk* (-kk-), *Fuunid* (-uu- vocalic geminate)
  • Syllable simplicity: Predominantly (C)V(C)
  • Vowel distribution: /a/ and /i/ predominate; /o/ and /u/ less common
  • Expanded consonant inventory: New Midnight data adds /ʒ/ (*Zhakir*), /tʃ/ (*Chonon*, *Chua*), /θ/ (*Har'athir*, *Dreth'amar*), /kw/ (*Har'kuai*), /dʒ/ (*Or'jan*)
  • Initial clusters: *Gn-* (*Gnarla*), *Ch-* (*Chonon*, *Chua*), *Zh-* (*Zhakir*), *Dr-* (*Dreth'amar*)

Relationship to Other Languages

Confirmed genetic relationship to the elven/troll family. The Haranir are confirmed by *The Art of Midnight* (pg. 125) to have split from the dark trolls/night elves ~16,000 BDP:

  • Related to elven: Deep genetic connection (confirmed dark troll ancestry), but no surface-level cognates with Darnassian/Thalassian/Nazja due to ~16,000 years of independent development
  • Related to troll (Zandali): No shared morphemes despite confirmed shared dark troll ancestry; Xhosa-accented English may reflect phonological substrate connection
  • Not titan-forged: Haranir are not titan-forged; *-dar* is likely borrowed from the Khaz Algar areal sphere
  • Not Aqir-descended: No confirmed shared morphemes with nerubian/qiraji; apostrophe convention likely independent or Blizzard aesthetic
Dark troll / night elf origin confirmed: *The Art of Midnight* (pg. 125) confirms: "those who would eventually become the haranir split from them [dark trolls/night elves] and journeyed deep below the ground." This makes Hara'ni a deeply divergent branch of the proto-troll/elven language family, separated by ~16,000 years of independent development. The confirmed translation of *Aln'hara* as "The Worldsoul of Azeroth," combined with the Haranir's deep connection to the Emerald Dream, World Tree roots, and druidic traditions, all align with this origin. However, Hara'ni shows NO obvious shared morphemes with Darnassian, Thalassian, Nazja, or Zandali — 16,000+ years of independent development may have erased cognate recognition. The Haranir use Xhosa-accented English like the Zandalari trolls, suggesting a possible phonological substrate connection.

Areal features shared with Khaz Algar languages:

  • The *-dar* toponymic suffix (shared with Earthen and Arathi, likely borrowed)
  • Medial apostrophe compounding (shared with nerubian, but widespread convention)

Haranir–Nerubian Geographic Proximity

Wildcamp Ul'ar is located near the City of Threads in Azj-Kahet, placing Haranir and nerubians in direct geographic proximity. The *-ar* element in *Ul'ar* parallels the nerubian *-ar* faction suffix (Anub'ar, Nerub'ar, Hath'ar). If *-ar* were a nerubian loanword, the semantic shift from "brood/collective" to "camp/settlement" would be natural. However, *-ar* is a common syllable, and no other Haranir–nerubian shared morphemes exist.

Confidence: Very Low for *-ar* as a nerubian loanword.

Greenspeaker / Thornspeaker Parallel

The Haranir title Greenspeaker (Na'layro) parallels the Drust title Thornspeaker (Ulfar et al.). Both follow the English compound pattern [Nature-element] + [speaker]. The parallel is most likely due to independent development: both cultures have druidic traditions, and the naming pattern is a natural English compound. Geographic and temporal separation (Drust in Kul Tiras ~2,600 years ago vs. Haranir in Harandar since ~16,000 BDP) makes contact borrowing extremely unlikely.

Confidence: Very Low for genuine in-universe connection; High for independent parallel development.
