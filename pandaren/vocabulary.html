Linguistic Contact Analysis

  • Substrate influence: The Mogu dominated Pandaren culture for millennia. Pandaren place names in the Vale of Eternal Blossoms and surrounding areas may preserve Mogu toponyms. Mogu'shan is the clearest example: the element -shan likely originates from Mogu, not Pandaren.
  • Loan direction: Given the power dynamic, most loanwords likely flowed from Mogu (dominant language) into Pandaren (substrate language). This is consistent with real-world patterns of linguistic borrowing under domination.
  • Sha vocabulary: The word Sha for the negative emotion entities in Mists of Pandaria may be related to Shath'Yar (Old God language) rather than either Pandaren or Mogu. The Sha are remnants of Y'Shaarj's power, which predates both races.
  • Titan vocabulary: Both Pandaren and Mogu share exposure to Titan facilities in Pandaria (Mogu'shan Vaults, Terrace of Endless Spring). Some place names may have Titan-language origins that predate both peoples.
  • Zandali influence: The Zandalari trolls invaded Pandaria and had extensive contact with both Mogu and Pandaren. The element -jin may be a Zandali borrowing present in both languages.

Methodological caveat: Both Pandaren and Mogu have extremely limited attestation. Cross-referencing two poorly attested languages multiplies uncertainty. The connections above are hypotheses, not confirmed relationships.
