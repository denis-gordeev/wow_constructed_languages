Phonological Observations

  • Tone: Romanized Pandaren names suggest a tonal language, consistent with Chinese cultural inspiration. Tone cannot be confirmed from romanized text alone.
  • Syllable structure: Predominantly (C)V(N) — simple open or lightly closed syllables (Shen, Jin, Xuen, Su).
  • Apostrophe usage: Yu'lon contains an apostrophe, possibly marking a morpheme boundary or glottal stop.
  • Hyphen usage: Shen-zin Su uses hyphens, possibly marking phrase boundaries.
  • Name reduplication: Li Li demonstrates reduplication, consistent with East Asian language patterns.
