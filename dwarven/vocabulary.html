Note on Dwarven vocabulary: Unlike Orcish or Darnassian, very few Dwarven words are directly attested in WoW canon. Most Dwarven "vocabulary" consists of proper names (Khaz Modan, Dun Morogh, Grim Batol) from which morphemes can be inferred. The Titan-prefix Uld- (Ulduar, Uldum, Uldaman) is not Dwarven in origin but is included because the Earthen — the Titan-created progenitors of Dwarves — were shaped in these facilities. The name element Khaz in Khaz Modan derives from the Titan Khaz'goroth, who shaped the Earthen.
