## Titan-Dwarven Cross-Reference

Dwarves descend from the Earthen, titan-forged beings created by Khaz'goroth. The Dwarven language retains several Titan morphemes, though 10,000+ years of divergence and the Curse of Flesh have obscured many connections.

| Dwarven Element | Titan Cognate | Shared Meaning | Evidence |
|-----------------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| Khaz | Khaz (in *Khaz'goroth*, *Khaz'mul*, *Khaz Algar*) | Forge / craft / shape | *Khaz'goroth* = "Shaper and Forger of Worlds"; *Khaz Modan* = "Mountain of Khaz" |
| -dun | (uncertain; possibly related to Titan *-den* as in *Ra-den*) | Fortification / settlement | *Bael'dun*, *Dun Morogh*, *Dun Modr*; Titan *Ra-den* uses *-den* as honorific |
| Uld- | Uld- (in *Ulduar*, *Uldum*, *Uldaman*, *Uldir*, etc.) | City / facility / titan place | Loaned directly from Titan; Brann Bronzebeard proposed *Uld* = "city" |
| -modan | -dor (in Titan *Kalimdor* = "Land of Eternal Starlight") | Mountain / land / kingdom | Possible sound correspondence: Titan *-dor* → Dwarven *-modan*; both relate to land/terrain |
| Earthen | (Titan term for stone-created beings) | Made of earth / stone | Direct loan from Titan; the Dwarven progenitors' name |

### Key Linguistic Observations from The War Within

1. **Khaz Algar confirms *Khaz-* as a productive Titan-derived toponymic prefix.** With *Khaz Modan* (Dwarven kingdom) and *Khaz Algar* (subterranean continent), the *Khaz-* prefix now appears in three distinct place names, strengthening the inference that *Khaz-* = "forge/craft/shape" in the Titan-forged language family.

2. **Earthen personal names differ from Dwarven personal names.** Dwarven names (Magni, Muradin, Brann, Moira) follow a Celtic/Norse short-name pattern. Earthen names (Baelgrim, Merrix, Adelgonn, Brinthe, Eirich, Lufsela) show a different pattern with longer, more complex names. This may reflect:
   - Earthen retaining older titan-forged naming conventions while Dwarves developed simpler names after the Curse of Flesh
   - Regional variation: Khaz Algar Earthen were isolated for millennia from Eastern Kingdoms Dwarves
   - Blizzard design choice for TWW aesthetics

3. **The *Bael-* element appears in both Earthen and Dwarven naming.** Earthen *Baelgrim* (Stormward) and Dwarven *Bael'dun* (dig site) share the *Bael-* root. If *Bael-* = "stone/earth," this demonstrates a shared morpheme between the two groups, consistent with their common Earthen ancestry. However, the RPG translation of *Bael Modan* = "Red Mountain" would suggest *Bael-* = "red" instead. This remains unresolved.

4. **The *Eir-* element may connect Earthen and Vrykul.** Earthen *Eirich* and Vrykul *Eyir* (Val'kyr goddess) both contain the *Eir-* element. In Norse mythology, *Eir* is a goddess of healing. If both languages preserve this as a Titan morpheme, it would demonstrate deep vocabulary retention across the titan-forged family.

5. **Skardyn as a void-corrupted Earthen term.** The *Skar-* element in *Skardyn* parallels Vrykul *Skar-* in *Skarvald* and *Voldskar*. If *Skar-* = "dark/corrupt" or "shadow" in the titan-forged family, this would be a meaningful connection, though the sample size is too small to confirm.

6. **The *-gaz* element appears in four Khaz Algar toponyms.** *Gundargaz*, *Nibelgaz*, *Dhar Durgaz*, and *Dun Algaz* all share the *-gaz* ending. Three of four are associated with mines or excavation areas, suggesting *-gaz* may mean "mine" or "dig" — a productive Earthen/Dwarven place-name morpheme. The fourth (*Gundargaz*) is a settlement, possibly named after nearby mines. This is the strongest new morpheme candidate from TWW.

7. **The *Dhar-* prefix appears in four Isle of Dorn toponyms.** *Dhar Oztan*, *Dhar Kazhad*, *Dhar Durgaz*, and *Dhar Bandar* all use *Dhar-* as a place prefix. All four refer to elevated or mountainous areas, suggesting *Dhar-* = "peak" or "mountain." If *Dhar-* = *-dar* = *-dor* (with metathesis), this would extend the *-dor* morpheme family with a specifically elevated meaning.

8. **Dornogal named after watchers Dornic and Galan.** The wiki confirms that Dornogal was "named in honor of the titanic watchers Dornic and Galan." This provides a possible etymology: *Dorn-* (from Dornic) + *-ogal* (possibly from *Galan*, reversed/mutated). If *Dornogal* = *Dorn* + *Gal* (a compound of two watcher names), the *-ogal* suffix analysis would be incorrect — it would be a name compound, not a morphological suffix.

9. **Confirmed Dwarven vocabulary includes 6+ translated words.** The Dwarven language page on warcraft.wiki.gg confirms: *Draig'cyfaill* = "Dragonheart", *Dwyar'hun* = "Star bow", *Gwyarbrawden* = warrior ritual, *Khadgar* = "Trust", *Khaz Modan* = "Mountain of Khaz", *Magna* = "Protector". This significantly expands the confirmed Dwarven lexicon beyond mere toponyms.
