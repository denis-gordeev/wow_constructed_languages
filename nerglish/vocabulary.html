Etymological Speculation: "Kobyss"

  1. Real-world "abyss": kobyss ≈ "co-abyss" or "of the abyss" — a name given by the Arathi based on the creatures' emergence from deep waters
  2. Ankoan-language origin: If kobyss descend from ankoan, Ko-byss could be a two-syllable ankoan word, but /ks/ cluster in -byss doesn't match typical ankoan phonotactics
  3. Nerglish substrate: The -yss ending could reflect gurgling phonology, but ko- is not typical of Nerglish onsets
  4. Independent Arathi designation: "Kobyss" may simply be an Arathi word, unrelated to any known language family
Confidence: Very Low — no native kobyss words attested.

Cross-Nerubian Contact

The Gutter tribe inhabits the Salien Gutters in the City of Threads (nerubian territory in Azj-Kahet). The nerubian alchemist Al'kubian studied this tribe for weeks, noting they grew stronger by consuming Ascended nerubians. This provides the only known direct linguistic contact between kobyss and nerubians.
