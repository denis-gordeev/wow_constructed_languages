Nerglish (Murloc/Jinyu/Ankoan/Kobyss) — Description

Speakers

Nerglish is the language of murlocs — ancient aquatic creatures found across Azeroth's coastlines and waterways. It is also spoken by makrura (lobster-people). The name "Nerglish" covers a language family that spans four evolutionary stages:

  • Murlocs speak Nerglish proper — a gurgling, consonant-heavy language with syllabic consonants
  • Jinyu evolved from murlocs exposed to the Vale of Eternal Blossoms' magic, developing dramatically increased phonological complexity
  • Ankoan descend from jinyu who left Pandaria for the deep ocean, with moderate simplification
  • Kobyss are ankoan-descended but show near-total language loss, retaining only 3–5 Common words — the only known case of linguistic regression in WoW

This places the Nerglish family entirely separate from both the titan-forged family and the Aqir-descended family. The family spans at least three evolutionary stages of increasing then decreasing linguistic complexity.

History

Murlocs are among the oldest races on Azeroth, predating the Titans' arrival. Their language, Nerglish, is correspondingly ancient. The Winterfin murlocs of Borean Tundra speak a dialect different enough from standard Nerglish to be mutually unintelligible.

The jinyu represent a dramatic linguistic leap: murlocs exposed to the Vale of Eternal Blossoms' magic had their "minds expanded and bodies grown," producing a language with full vowel inventories, CV/CVC syllable structure, and complex proper names — a complete transformation from Nerglish's gurgling phonology.

The ankoan represent a moderate simplification from jinyu, with shorter names and no apostrophe usage.

The kobyss represent the only known case of linguistic regression in WoW. While the murloc → jinyu transition was driven by magical enhancement, the ankoan → kobyss transition appears driven by environmental and cultural factors (Undersea isolation, Void corruption, cultural degeneration from millennial warfare with naga).

Cultural Context

  • Murlocs are fiercely territorial and tribal, with clan identity expressed through Nerglish vocalizations
  • Jinyu are contemplative and spiritual, with titles like Waterspeaker and Pearlkeeper reflecting their aquatic religious practice
  • Ankoan are a warrior people who took the fight to Queen Azshara, naming their clan Waveblade
  • Kobyss are vicious ambush predators who attack Arathi settlements during Beledar's Void phase, using only violence-related Common phrases

Relationship to Other Languages

  • No confirmed genetic relationship to any other Azerothian language family
  • The Nerglish family is entirely separate from both the titan-forged languages and the Aqir-descended languages
  • Murloc names sometimes appear in Titan-forged contexts (e.g., Glibb in Earthen vocabulary) but these are borrowings, not cognates
  • The kobyss Gutter tribe has documented contact with nerubians (the alchemist Al'kubian studied them), but no linguistic borrowing is attested
